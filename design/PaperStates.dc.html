Option A — the three states

The die carries the verdict. The slip carries the words.

1 · Resting
8

The die shows the 8. No slip yet — but its space is held open, so the answer does not shove the button down the page.

2 · Turning

Ink settles for 1.5s. Button reads Asking… and is disabled; shake is ignored until it lands.

3 · Answered
Maybe

Die stamps Yes, Maybe or No — short, so it survives translation. The slip fades up with the full sentence.

Verdict groups

10 affirmative → Yes · 5 non-committal → Maybe · 5 negative → No. The canonical split, unchanged.

Accessibility

Slip is the live region, not the die. Focus ring is a 2px ink outline offset 3px. Reduced motion swaps the turn for a fade.
